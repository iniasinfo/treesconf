The TREES Experience

Why participate?

Every TREES conference is intended to create opportunities for learning, discussion and meaningful professional connections.

Present Your Research

Share research findings, emerging ideas, methodologies and practical work with an interdisciplinary audience.

Exchange Ideas

Engage in scientific discussion, ask questions and learn from perspectives beyond your immediate field.

Build Connections

Meet researchers, professionals, academics and innovators who may become future collaborators.

Learn & Develop

Discover new developments, approaches and perspectives that can inform your own work.

Think Internationally

Participate in a community designed to connect scientific and professional perspectives across borders.

Discover Opportunities

Conferences can open doors to new collaborations, research directions and professional relationships.

How TREES Conferences Work

From announcement to conversation.

STEP 01

Conference Announced

We announce the conference, scientific scope, dates and participation opportunities.

STEP 02

Abstracts & Submissions

Researchers and professionals can submit their work according to the published conference guidelines.

STEP 03

Programme

Accepted contributions and sessions are organized into a structured scientific programme.

STEP 04

Conference

Participants come together to present, discuss, learn and connect.
