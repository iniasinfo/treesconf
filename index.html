About TREES

A platform for knowledge to grow.

TREES is a new international conference initiative focused on Technology, Research, Engineering, & Empirical Sciences.

Our goal is simple: create spaces where people can present ideas, exchange knowledge, discover different perspectives and build relationships that continue beyond a conference.

We believe great conferences aren't simply about sitting in a room and listening to presentations. They are about the conversations that happen before, during and after them.

Why the name "TREES"?

A tree starts with a seed, grows through connection and eventually creates something much larger than itself.

We see research and innovation in much the same way: an idea can begin with one person and grow through collaboration.
