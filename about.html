About TREES

Growing a global scientific community.

TREES — Technology, Research, Engineering, & Empirical Sciences — is a new international conference initiative created to bring people, ideas and disciplines together.

  1. Home
  2. About
Our Beginning

Starting with an idea.

TREES was created around a simple belief: scientific progress becomes stronger when people have meaningful opportunities to share knowledge, question ideas and connect with one another.

The world of research is increasingly interconnected. Technology influences medicine. Engineering contributes to healthcare. Data science transforms scientific discovery. Biology inspires new technologies.

Yet many areas of research still operate within their own communities. TREES aims to create conference environments where these boundaries can become opportunities for collaboration.

We are starting small, building carefully and developing TREES with the long-term goal of creating a trusted international platform for scientific exchange.

From a seed to a community.

TREES is at the beginning of its journey. We don't believe in presenting numbers or achievements that don't exist yet. Instead, we're focused on building the foundations properly — one conference, one conversation and one connection at a time.

The Name

What TREES stands for.

The name reflects the broad scientific foundation we want to build — bringing technology, research, engineering and empirical sciences into one growing international community.

T

Technology

Emerging technologies, computing, artificial intelligence, data science, digital systems and technological innovation.

R

Research

Scientific inquiry, discovery, academic research, evidence, methodologies and the pursuit of new knowledge.

E

Engineering

Engineering disciplines, applied sciences, practical innovation, design and solutions to real-world challenges.

ES

Empirical Sciences

Evidence-based scientific work across natural, life, medical, environmental and interdisciplinary fields.

Direction

Where we're going.

Our Vision

To grow into a trusted international platform where researchers, scientists, healthcare professionals, engineers, academics and innovators can exchange knowledge and discover opportunities for meaningful collaboration.

Our Mission

To organize thoughtful, accessible and academically focused conferences that encourage research presentation, scientific discussion, interdisciplinary exchange and professional connection.

What We Believe

Principles behind TREES.

As a new organization, our culture is being established from the beginning. These are the principles we want TREES to grow around.

Curiosity

Science begins with questions. We value curiosity, exploration and the willingness to investigate ideas that challenge what we already know.

Interdisciplinarity

Important problems rarely belong to one discipline. We encourage meaningful connections between fields and perspectives.

Integrity

Scientific communication should be built on honesty, transparency, responsible research and respect for evidence.

Community

Conferences are ultimately about people. We want to create environments where participants feel welcome to exchange ideas and connect.

Global Perspective

Research benefits from diverse geographic, cultural and institutional perspectives.

Continuous Growth

TREES itself is a growing initiative. We intend to learn from every conference and continuously improve what we build.

Our Scientific Scope

Broad enough to connect. Focused enough to matter.

TREES is intentionally designed as an interdisciplinary conference platform. Our areas of interest can include established scientific disciplines as well as emerging fields created at the intersection of different domains.

  • Technology and computing
  • Engineering and applied sciences
  • Medical and health sciences
  • Life and biological sciences
  • Environmental and sustainability sciences
  • Interdisciplinary and emerging fields

Connecting disciplines

The most interesting opportunities often appear where disciplines overlap. TREES aims to provide a place for those conversations to happen.

Our Conference Philosophy

More than a presentation.

A successful conference should create value before, during and after the event. Our approach is built around the complete experience of scientific exchange.

01 — SHARE

Present Ideas

Give researchers and professionals an opportunity to present their work, findings and perspectives.

02 — QUESTION

Encourage Discussion

Questions and critical discussion can be just as valuable as the presentation itself.

03 — CONNECT

Build Relationships

Create opportunities for participants to meet potential collaborators, colleagues and peers.

04 — GROW

Continue the Conversation

The conference should be a starting point for ideas and relationships, not the end.

A Fresh Start

We're building TREES from the ground up.

TREES doesn't have a long history yet. And that's intentional in how we present ourselves. We're not going to fill this website with invented numbers, speaker lists or achievements. We're starting with a clear purpose and building the organization step by step.
