About the Conference

Advancing conversations across medical science.

The International Conference on Medical & Health Sciences is envisioned as a multidisciplinary platform for sharing research, clinical perspectives, emerging technologies and scientific developments across healthcare.

The conference will bring together researchers, clinicians, healthcare professionals, academics, students and other professionals interested in advancing knowledge and improving understanding across medical and health-related disciplines.

The programme will be developed around scientific presentations, invited contributions, discussions and opportunities for professional interaction.

Who Should Attend?

  • Medical researchers and scientists
  • Physicians and healthcare professionals
  • Surgeons and clinical specialists
  • Academics and university faculty
  • Postgraduate and doctoral researchers
  • Healthcare technology professionals
  • Pharmaceutical and biotechnology researchers
  • Students and early-career researchers

Conference Objectives

  • Facilitate exchange of current scientific and clinical knowledge.
  • Encourage interdisciplinary collaboration.
  • Provide a platform for researchers to present their work.
  • Explore emerging developments in medical and health sciences.
  • Encourage meaningful scientific discussion and collaboration.

  • Cancer Biology

  • Therapeutics

Cardiovascular Sciences

  • Cardiology
